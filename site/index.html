The real problem

It's not effort.
It's not talent.

Tier 2 families average around $2,400 a season. AAA families average closer to $7,000, and elite travel programs regularly pass $10,000. The money isn't the problem. Almost none of it goes toward the three things that actually move a player.

01

No plan between sessions

Team practice is 60 minutes of standing in lines. A private lesson is an hour twice a month. That leaves 167 hours a week with no structure and no idea what to work on next.

02

No real feedback

"Compete harder." "Want it more." Nobody can act on that. Your player has never had his shot broken down frame by frame and been told which mechanic is costing him velocity.

03

Nobody coaches the mind

He trains hard and still disappears after a bad shift. Confidence, bounce-back and composure are trainable skills — and in youth hockey almost nobody is teaching them.

The flagship service

Shot mechanics,
broken down 7 ways

"Shoot harder" isn't coaching. Every shot is seven mechanics stacked on each other, and a slow shot is almost always one or two of them failing. Here's the exact framework every video review is scored against.

POINT 01

Weight Transfer

Loading the back leg and driving through to the front. Hips lead the hands.

POINT 02

Stick Flex

Puck ahead of the blade, loading the shaft into the ice instead of slapping at it.

POINT 03

Release Timing

When the blade closes, and what he does before it. Goalies read telegraphed shots.

POINT 04

Hand Positioning

Bottom hand drives, top hand pulls. Separation changes with the shot type.

POINT 05

Follow Through

Where the blade finishes decides where the puck goes.

POINT 06

Balance

A stable base through the release. Falling away leaks the power he just loaded.

POINT 07

Shooting Posture

Athletic knee bend, chest up, shoulders square to intent.

THE OUTPUT

A score out of 70

Every submission returns a score per point, the two to fix first, and three drills that fix them.

Case study 02 · Shot rebuild

Elite flex, slow release — the real diagnosis

A full 7-point breakdown found stick flex at 9/10 and weight transfer at 8/10 — but release timing at 5/10, with a 0.8-second puck drag before release. The fix wasn't a harder shot. It was a shorter runway. That's the kind of finding that changes what a player trains for the next 30 days.

Straight answers

Hockey training FAQ

What age players do you train?
Ages 10 to 18. Below about 12, a parent should work through the plan alongside the player — the drills are simple but the mindset lessons land better with a conversation attached. From 13 up, most players run it themselves, and honestly that's part of the point.
How much is an on-ice session?
$149 per session, on the ice at The Capital Clubhouse in Waldorf. There's no set time limit — a session runs as long as it needs to instead of ending on a clock. When we're both available you get both coaches on the ice, and we keep session sizes small on purpose so every player gets real attention and real reps. Details here.
Where in Waldorf, Maryland do you train?
All in-person sessions run at The Capital Clubhouse, 3033 Waldorf Market Place, Waldorf, MD 20603 — our only training location. It's just off Route 301, with free on-site parking, and it serves families across Waldorf, White Plains, La Plata, Brandywine, Charles County and the wider Southern Maryland area. Directions and details here.
Do we have to travel to you?
Only if you want in-person ice time. The core of the program — video analysis, the weekly plan and mindset work — is built to run remotely, so families outside Southern Maryland get the same system without the drive.
Who will actually be coaching my player?
One of two named coaches, never an anonymous reviewer. We're both coaches and both currently playing Junior A — the difference is that one of us owns the business and sets the system we both coach. Coach Brayden works with players directly, alongside Coach Jack, our development coach, skill specialist and NCAA prospect. Meet them both here.
What is mindset development training?
It's the mental side of the game, coached deliberately rather than left to chance — confidence building, mental toughness, handling mistakes mid-game, performing under pressure, goal setting, accountability, winning habits, discipline, game preparation routines and leadership. It's included with the Premium program.
Will this get my son to Junior or college hockey?
No one can promise that, and anyone who does isn't being straight with you. Fewer than 0.11% of youth players reach the NHL, and Junior and college spots are genuinely scarce. What we do promise: better shot mechanics, a real weekly plan, trained habits, and a player who handles a bad game better. Those are the things inside your control.
He already has a skills coach. Is this redundant?
The opposite. Your skills coach gets him for an hour, twice a month. This is what he does the other twenty-nine days — and it means he shows up to that lesson already knowing which two mechanics he's working on. It makes money you already spend go further.
What do we need to film a video analysis?
A phone. Two angles — one from the side at about hip height, one from the front, roughly ten feet away, with his whole body in frame. Five shots minimum of the same type. There's a walkthrough in your onboarding, and if the footage isn't usable we'll tell you exactly what to change at no charge.
How quickly do you respond?
We respond to all inquiries within 24 hours. Premium members get priority support and video breakdowns returned inside 72 hours — most come back in under 48.
Does this work for defencemen and goalies?
Mindset, Systems, Habits and Leadership apply to every position — four of the Six Pillars. Shot mechanics content covers forwards and defencemen, including a separate point-shot module. Goalie-specific technical coaching isn't offered yet, so if he's a goalie, know what you're getting.
Can our team or association work with you?
Yes. Team and association packages are quoted per roster and include a live mindset session with the team. Request a custom quote with your roster size and level.
