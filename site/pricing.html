Pricing FAQ

How much is an on-ice session, and how long is it?
On-ice sessions are $149 each at The Capital Clubhouse in Waldorf. There's no set time limit — the session runs as long as it needs to rather than ending on a timer. When both coaches are available you get both of us on the ice, and we deliberately keep session sizes small so every player gets real attention and real reps instead of standing in a line.
What does the setup fee cover?
The intake assessment, your player's baseline video breakdown scored against all seven mechanics points, and building the first custom training plan. It's the heaviest work of the whole engagement and it happens once.
Am I locked into a contract?
No. The monthly is month-to-month and you can cancel any time. The setup fee is one-time and non-recurring.
Can I switch plans later?
Yes. Moving from Basic to Premium only costs the difference in setup fees, not a second full setup.
Do you offer sibling or team rates?
Yes — both. Request a custom quote with the number of players and we'll price it properly.
What if it isn't a fit?
Tell us within the first 14 days and we'll refund the monthly fee, no interrogation. We'd rather have an honest conversation than an unhappy family.

Ready when you are

Free assessment, honest answer, no pressure. If we're not the right fit we'll say so.
